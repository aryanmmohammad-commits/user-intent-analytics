# Business Context

You are an Analytics Engineer supporting a SaaS developer platform similar to CircleCI.

Teams:
- Product wants to understand meaningful product behavior.
- Sales wants future qualified-user signals.
- Revenue wants reliable customer/product metrics.
- Analytics Engineering owns the trusted transformation layer.

Project 01 scope:
- Understand raw product activity.
- Standardize events.
- Build reliable user and organization behavior models.
- Prepare a foundation for Project 02.

Do not assume that every event equals commercial intent.
Intent is a later analytical interpretation that must be validated against business outcomes.
